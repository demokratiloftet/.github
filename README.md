# A shared toolbox for public spheres

We require media technologies for many things in our daily lives, and technology is an indispensable part of how we participate in public life, how we form opinions and organize around causes we care about. Yet, many of these things have been hijacked by platforms that are poor supports for this, platforms that even damage the public spheres.

We set out to play a part in helping building a toolbox that can strengthen democratic discourse. This effort is grounded in Norway, and "løftet" is a Norwegian word with dual meanings: It can mean *lifting*, that we are lifting democracy up. It can also mean *promise*, this is our promise, to work together to build technology that can not only support democracy, but also provide new ways for democracy to steer technology.

## What we do

The project was launched in September 2026, and we seek to build several components that can be reused for many purposes. They will be FLOSS software that conforms to the [Digital Public Goods Standard](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/standard).

We are a little vague right now, because we are still working out our priorities. There are many things that are assumed by law to exist, but that aren't. For example, interoperability and data portability is regulated in European law, but isn't operationalized to the extent needed. They will be important to us.

We will focus on trust, and therefore social verification is important as a first step, but also verification of person's roles and qualification.

Finally, we aim to make sure that there exists moderation frameworks that are accountable to Norwegian law.

Out focus will be mainly on systems for use in the [Atmosphere](https://atproto.com/).

## Who is behind this

The [Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA)](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/), the [Fritt Ord Foundation](https://frittord.no/en/home), the [Norwegian Confederation for Sports (NIF)](https://www.idrettsforbundet.no/), and [SimulaMet](https://www.simulamet.no/) AI Safety Department. The [Directorate of Digitalisation](https://www.digdir.no/) contributes coordination towards existing initiatives and solutions under the auspices of the public sector. The technological development work is carried out by [Devotta](https://devotta.no/), who has extensive experience with the global digital common good DHIS2.

## Why we are not doing *that*?

It is natural to ask why not work on ActivityPub. We align with the [open declaration on the European Social Stack](https://european.social/) which has signatories from across several communities, and note that AT Protocol, Activity Pub and Matrix has three different but overlapping strengths. At present, we think that AT Protocol has the most impetus for becoming an important protocol for the larger public spheres. This is not to the exclusion of the others, and it is likely that work we do will also benefit the others, but at present, we have decided that the atmosphere is best suited for our efforts.
